raw     = mutation points recorded for this cell
notRun  = real mutation points the benchmark's wall-clock budget never reached. Excluded from every rate.
genKill = the Halide compiler itself rejected the mutant -- a kill category only staged compilation produces
equiv   = emitted .stmt byte-identical to baseline: unreachable at target=host, unkillable by any test kind. Proven equivalent, so RESOLVED.
eff     = effective (reachable, code-changing) mutants. Kind counts are over eff, never over raw.
t1/t2gold/t2writ/t3perf = mutants killed by that test kind. A mutant may appear under several; 'any' is the union.
resolvd = (killed by any kind + proven equivalent) / (eff + equiv)
